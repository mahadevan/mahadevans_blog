# Mahadevan Iyer

I'm a senior software engineer working where systems, applied AI, and games meet.

My path has run through backend engineering and AI, and it now leads into game development. What has stayed constant is the work I enjoy most: understanding a messy problem, designing a clean way through it, and shipping something people can actually use. Tools and frameworks keep changing; judgement, taste, and the habit of finishing things don't.

I'm currently building **Mayaverse**, an independent game project. It brings together the parts of engineering I care about — architecture, interaction, narrative, and the craft of making an idea feel alive.

I write here about software, AI, games, and the process of building from scratch — including the false starts and the lessons that only arrive after you ship.
